How it works

A clear automation flow from input to action.

The system structure stays understandable: detect a condition, apply the agreed logic, coordinate devices, then make status visible to the user.

01 / Sense

Detect

Occupancy, time, environmental inputs or user commands provide the trigger.

02 / Coordinate

Decide

The agreed routine determines what should happen across connected systems.

03 / Execute

Act

Lighting, climate, curtains or compatible devices respond as configured.

04 / Feedback

Review

Status, alerts and relevant information are presented through the selected interface.
