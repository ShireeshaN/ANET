What the BMS does

A Building Management System creates a single operating layer for building services — helping facility teams monitor status, coordinate schedules, review selected conditions and respond faster when something needs attention.

VisibilityCentral view across services and zones
CoordinationRelated systems working with shared logic
SchedulingTime-based operations and control rules
ResponseAlerts, status review and operator action
