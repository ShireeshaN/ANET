Automation domains

Six operational layers. One coordinated workplace.

The core commercial automation scope is organized around occupancy, schedules, climate, meeting rooms, access and facility visibility — so the system remains clear instead of becoming a collection of disconnected controls.

01 / Occupancy

Occupancy-based control

Coordinate compatible lighting and comfort systems with real space usage, reducing unnecessary operation while preserving user comfort.

02 / Lighting

Lighting schedules & zones

Manage work areas, reception zones, shared spaces, cleaning periods and after-hours operation independently.

03 / Climate

Climate coordination

Align compatible HVAC controls with occupancy, schedules and operational requirements.

04 / Meeting rooms

Meeting-room scenes

Bring presentation, lighting and shading controls into clear one-touch room modes.

05 / Access

Access integration

Coordinate approved access events with relevant space controls and selected operational actions.

06 / Visibility

Facility visibility

Provide facility teams with a clear view of connected zones, selected events, operating state and the information needed for the next action.

Role-based control

The right control for the right user.

Commercial automation becomes more useful when different users see only the information and actions relevant to their responsibilities.

01 / Facility team

Operational overview

Support daily building operation with visibility across agreed zones and events.

  • Zone status and schedules
  • Selected alerts and events
  • Operational overrides
  • Review of connected systems
02 / Workspace users

Simple room control

  • Meeting-room scenes
  • Lighting and shading actions
  • Selected temperature adjustment
  • Simple start / end modes

Automation logic

From occupancy or schedule to coordinated action.

The operational flow stays understandable: recognize a condition, apply the agreed rule, coordinate systems, then surface the result to the people who need it.

01

Sense

Occupancy, time, access events or user input provide the operational trigger.

02

Apply logic

The configured schedule, room state or workflow determines the required response.

03

Coordinate

Lighting, climate, meeting-room functions and compatible systems respond together.

04

Review

Status and relevant events become visible through the selected facility interface.
