04 / GOOD TO KNOW

Clarity before
you begin.

What do you need to prepare a proposal?

Share your location, drawings or site photos, intended use, timeline and any existing equipment details. For bms with iot, we will also confirm the required zones, interfaces and operating preferences.

Can the existing system be retained?

The existing BMS can remain where its interfaces and operating condition support the agreed integration. An audit confirms accessible points, protocol support, licences and any required changes.

What is included in the final scope?

The proposal identifies equipment, installation responsibilities, integration boundaries, documentation and acceptance checks. Optional additions and dependencies are agreed before implementation.
