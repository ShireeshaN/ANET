Layered design

Every residential scheme works better when the light is layered correctly.

We shape the atmosphere of a home through a clear balance of ambient, task and accent lighting — so each room feels beautiful, practical and easy to live in.

01

Ambient lighting

The comfortable base layer that gives a room overall brightness and visual calm through cove lighting, recessed fixtures, concealed strips and ceiling illumination.

02

Task lighting

Focused lighting for activities like cooking, reading, dressing, washing and working — giving clarity exactly where the home needs it.

03

Accent lighting

Decorative and feature lighting that adds depth, highlights materials, draws attention to details and creates a premium evening identity.
