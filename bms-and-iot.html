One project. Two coordinated layers.

BMS handles the building. IoT extends the intelligence.

The difference here is important: both layers are planned together from the beginning, so local building operation and wider data visibility work as one architecture rather than as separate add-ons.

BMS control layer

The BMS remains the building’s operational core — coordinating plant, schedules, alarms and real-time local control across connected services.

LightingSchedules, scenes and occupancy logic
EnergyMetering and local operational visibility
Water / UtilitiesPumps, tanks and service status

Data journey

Clear local control. Structured digital visibility.

The BMS and IoT layers should each do what they do best, with a clean handoff between building operation and higher-level insight.

How the information moves.

Use the tabs to communicate the operating model: data is captured, controlled locally, normalized at the edge and then surfaced as useful insight.

Sense

Capture building conditions

Field devices and meters provide the live information needed for local control and wider visibility.

Control

Operate locally through the BMS

Schedules, setpoints, sequences and building logic remain close to the building and continue working locally.

Connect

Normalize and move data through the edge

Supported protocols and data points pass through the IoT gateway into a structured, secure data layer.

Understand

Turn data into building insight

Dashboards, analytics, notifications and portfolio views help teams understand what is happening across one or many buildings.
