Daily control cycle

From startup to review.

Use this section to explain how the automation environment supports real operations across a working shift.

06:00

Startup readiness

Panels energize, machine states are checked, permissives are confirmed and the process sequence prepares for operation.

09:00

Production coordination

PLC logic, drives, field sensors and operator stations keep the line synchronized with process demand.

13:00

Supervision & response

SCADA and HMI stations provide alarm visibility, trend review and operator action during the live process window.

18:00

Shift review

Selected logs, events, runtime data and energy indicators help the team review performance and prepare the next cycle.
