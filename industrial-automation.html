04 / GOOD TO KNOW

Clarity before
you begin.

What do you need to prepare a proposal?

Share your location, drawings or site photos, intended use, timeline and any existing equipment details. For industrial automation, we will also confirm the required zones, interfaces and operating preferences.

Can this be installed in an existing space?

Existing spaces can be assessed for an upgrade. Cabling, available power, mounting access and equipment compatibility determine what can be retained and what needs changing.

What is included in the final scope?

The proposal identifies equipment, installation responsibilities, integration boundaries, documentation and acceptance checks. Optional additions and dependencies are agreed before implementation.
